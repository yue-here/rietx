Benchmark · powder X-ray diffraction

Same fit, two orders of magnitude fewer parameters

pxrdref refined against WPEM on the datasets published with AI-Driven Structure Refinement of X-ray Diffraction (arXiv:2602.16372). The agreement factors turn out to be the same arithmetic — verified, not assumed. The models behind them differ by a factor of 67.

Datasets: github.com/Bin-Cao/PyWPEM/tree/main/CASES. Structures from the Crystallography Open Database. Every number on this page is read from studies/wpem_bench/results/*.json, written by the run scripts in that directory — nothing here is transcribed by hand.
