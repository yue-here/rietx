# The magnetic structure index

Load it when you are about to determine a magnetic structure: a converged neutron fit leaves intensity unexplained and you want the candidate models ranked rather than one stated by hand.

*A reference file of the `rietx` skill. The body it belongs to is
[`SKILL.md`](../SKILL.md). Generated by `docs/skill/make_api_index.py` from the
installed package, like [`api.md`](api.md), and loaded only by a session doing
this; everything in `rx.` is `import rietx as rx`.*

## Determining a magnetic structure

`rx.solve_magnetic(ref, data, sites=[...], ion=...)` takes a converged **neutron** fit and proposes a propagation vector, candidate models per k, one trial refinement per class the powder cannot separate, and a ranked list with a stated criterion: ΔBIC against a nuclear reference, which charges each extra amplitude, and never Rwp, since more amplitudes always fit better. Classes inside the tie width abstain together rather than name a winner, listed fewest moment parameters first, and a class with no supported moment cannot win. A non-neutron histogram is refused by name; everything else, "nothing to solve" included, is reported. Read `MagneticSolution.verdict` before `.best`, and `references/magnetic.md` for what each reading must not be taken to mean. Provisional: the criterion may still move.

## Displacive mode amplitudes

`mode_amplitudes(parent, child, (P, p))` from `rietx.crystallography.representation.distortion` decomposes a distorted child `Phase` (r_parent = P·r_child + p) into its parent's irreps, in Å: AMPLIMODES's unit, Σ mult·|ε|² = 1 over the child's primitive cell, in the unstrained reference metric Pᵀ·G_parent·P (`reference="average"` takes the parent-symmetric part of the child's cell, `"parent"` the parent's own). Every irrep the child admits is returned, secondary and Γ-point ones included, with the uniform translation removed, so Σ A_τ² is the field's squared norm and Σ `n_modes` its free coordinates. `irrep` is this package's small-irrep label: compare a published table on `isotropy_group`. `distortion_basis` returns the modes alone, per irrep. Provisional: refining them is a later WP-1419 chunk.

- `rietx.crystallography.representation.distortion.mode_amplitudes(parent, child, transform, *, reference: str = 'average', tolerance: float = 1.5) -> ModeDecomposition` — Decompose a child structure into `A_τ` per (k, irrep, direction), in Å.
- `rietx.crystallography.representation.distortion.ModeDecomposition` — A child decomposed against its parent into `A_τ` per (k, irrep, direction).
  Fields: `rows: tuple[ModeAmplitude, ...]`, `total: float`, `residual: float`, `translation: tuple[float, float, float]`, `displacements: np.ndarray`, `basis: ModeBasis`
- `rietx.crystallography.representation.distortion.ModeAmplitude` — One irrep's share of a child's distortion, in Å.
  Fields: `k: tuple[str, str, str]`, `irrep: str`, `isotropy_group: str`, `primary: bool | None`, `n_modes: int`, `amplitude: float`, `mode_amplitudes: tuple[float, ...]`
- `rietx.crystallography.representation.distortion.distortion_basis(parent, child, transform, *, reference: str = 'average') -> ModeBasis` — Every symmetry-adapted mode a child of `parent` admits, in Å.

## Displacive candidates of a parent

`distortion_candidates(parent, k, sites=None)` from `rietx.crystallography.magnetic.isotropy` lists every child a displacement at `k` can give: one per (irrep, direction) of `candidates(kind="displacive")` over the named sites (all by default), the child `Phase` in a standard setting with (P, p), and its `distortion_basis`, every irrep the child admits, in Å. `driving` is the candidate's own irrep, `rest` the others. The k fence (2k in the reciprocal lattice) is `candidates`'. A candidate whose child the probe refuses still comes back, with `child` `None` and `refusal` set (its `code`, `message` and each draw's operation count): `DISTORTION_CHILD_LARGER` (the atoms cannot realise the group), `_SMALLER` (a tolerance miss) or `_UNIDENTIFIED`.

- `rietx.crystallography.magnetic.isotropy.distortion_candidates(parent, k, sites=None) -> tuple[DistortionCandidate, ...]` — Every displacive candidate of `parent` at `k`, with its child and modes in Å.
- `rietx.crystallography.magnetic.isotropy.DistortionCandidate` — One displacive candidate of a parent: its child, (P, p) and its modes in Å.
  Fields: `candidate: MagneticCandidate`, `child: object`, `transform: tuple[np.ndarray, np.ndarray] | None`, `basis: object`, `sites: tuple[str, ...]`, `refusal: DistortionRefusal | None = None`
