## Goal

Every argument of every call on `reference/api-techniques.md`, and the two calls on
`reference/api-types.md`, has a numpy-style Parameters entry, rendered under its signature.
When this and WP-2025 close, WP-2024's `UNDOCUMENTED_PARAMETERS` baseline is empty and the
gate's "every argument documented" half holds with no exceptions.
