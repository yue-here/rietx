**The macOS split in the QPA guide.** The nightly's macOS job is not a gate,
but on `3e0dc6f7` it printed CaF₂ 3.08 ± 0.21 wt% from
`examples/guides/qpa.py`'s first fit, where the page quotes ± 2.24. Linux
prints 2.24. So does a local arm64 Mac (darwin 25.6), both with numpy 2.4.6 and
scipy 1.17.1 on Python 3.12 and with the runner's numpy 2.5.4, scipy 1.18.1 and
Python 3.13 (`6fe7a5bb` snapshot). The runner image was `macos-26-arm64`,
macOS 26.6.2. Measured locally on that fit: `phases.1.extinction` 1361 ± 2116
(`stderr_uncorrected` 242, so the Bérar-Lelann factor is 8.7), and no stage
took a LAPACK fallback. Dropping that factor alone would print ± 0.26, so it
is not the whole story. The fraction agrees on every platform. Only its esd
differs, by 10.7×.

The 2026-10-22 macOS nightly (`d01ae735`) passed the QPA guide, but its tree
did not yet hold WP-2002's `e5976784`, which re-pinned the page for the
closing stage. So the split first shows on a tree holding #1085 (WP-2002's
closing stage, esd reporting and `optimize/lapack.py`). 1.9.0 has no QPA guide,
so whether 1.9.0's same fit splits is unmeasured.

**What the runner showed, 2026-10-24** (run 40083266263, branch
`tmp/wp2043-macos-qpa`, never merged: the fit above, Python 3.13, numpy 2.5.4,
scipy 1.18.1, compiled tier on and off alike). 1.9.0 gives CaF₂ 3.09 ± 2.32
wt% and extinction 1361 ± 2181 on both `macos-26-arm64` and Linux. This tree
gives 3.08 ± 2.24 and 1361 ± 2116 on Linux, and 3.08 ± 0.21 and 1361 ± 0.007
on macOS. Only three esds of 56 differ: extinction (×3.3e-6), CaF₂'s scale
(×0.093) and Ca's Biso (×0.56). So the split is new in 1.10's numbers.

**Its cause predates 1.10.** `model/extinction.py` copies GSAS-II's Laue
factor (`GetPwdrExt`, checked in GSAS-II 5.8.4 source this session): the
six-term series on 0 < x ≤ 1 and the two-term asymptote above, which meet with
a step, E_L(1⁻) = 0.67420 and E_L(1⁺) = 0.69815. The module's docstring says
real powder data keep x ≪ 1. On this fit, at the answer, CaF₂'s 11 reflections
reach x = 6.49, five have x > 1, and the one at 2θ 19.004° has x = 1.0000. The
solver parks extinction where a reflection touches the step, in 1.9.0
(1361.11) as on this tree (1361.15). The extinction column is a forward
difference at h = 1e-6 relative (0.00136). At the answer it agrees with a
central difference at 1e-6 (norm 0.1957, cosine 1.0). A central difference at
1e-4 straddles the step: norm 22.47, cosine −0.19. Earlier closing-stage
iterates gave the solver a column of norm 4503. A forward step that straddles
the step reads jump/h, so an esd near zero is the expected symptom wherever the
last Jacobian straddles. Which platform does is a matter of rounding.

**The continuous alternative.** e^(−x)·[I₀(x) + I₁(x)] expands to Sabine's
series c₁…c₄ and c₅ = −7/640 and to his four-term asymptote, by this session's
own expansion (the paper is not in the corpus). Against GSAS-II's branches it
differs by 4e-8 at x = 0.25, 5.6e-6 at 0.5, 7.9e-4 at 1⁻, 3.6e-2 at 1⁺, 9.8e-3
at 2, 1.1e-3 at 5. Patched in on this fit (numpy only, a prototype):
extinction 1919 ± 4066, CaF₂ 3.58 ± 3.53 wt%, Rwp 0.08163 against 0.08156,
and HIGH_CORRELATION scale ~ extinction at ρ = +0.998 fires where it did not.
The held fit is unchanged at 1.37 ± 0.07.
