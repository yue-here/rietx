## How v1.12 is getting here

### 1.11.0 cut and published (closed 2026-11-04)

The fifth weekly cut came eleven days after 1.10.0 and carried 183 merged pull requests.
The staged notes were far from complete. Of the pull requests that changed source and did not
touch the notes, 38 had no mention, and the cut wrote a note for each one a user can see.

Merging #1310 exposed a misreading. The cut had called tutorial 05's Rwp of 0.966 a deliberately
broken series in a stale notebook. It was the 2 % step, and bisection put the change at #1305.
The maintainer ruled that a noise check goes into 1.11 (WP-2086, #1406). The first report, a
series failing at 2 %, was one noise draw. Over 100 draws the reach is a rate, so the notes give
it as one, and they do not claim a plain fit 2 % off is restored.

The cut stayed on kernel wheel 1.3 by the maintainer's merge. `rietx-kernels` 1.4.0 is still
unpublished. Its only caller, `rietx.solve`, is unexported and has a numpy fallback, so no 1.11
user reaches the difference. WP-2043's ground, that nothing calls the 1.4 kernels, no longer holds
as written, and WP-1997 still owes the floor raise. Measured on a real 1.10.0 install, 1.10.0
refuses every structure, history log, refinement result and series result this release writes,
so a `.rex` project saved here does not open there. Tutorial 01's first pass fires
`STAGE_WINDOWS_STALE` with no prose to explain it, and the maintainer left that for later.

The maintainer's merge of #1400 approved the version, the notes and the tag. The nightly on that commit
passed every job, the tag went on it, and the maintainer approved `pypi`. The counts are in the
[v1.11 record's ship appendix](v1.11.md#appendix--acceptance-at-ship-2026-11-04).

### Main is green on every nightly leg (2036, closed 2026-11-04)

From 16 October the nightly was red or cancelled every night. The failures had four causes, and
they shared one acceptance: a nightly green on all six jobs. The Linux full suite stopped
finishing once the closing stage landed, because one hostile series' closing stage ran to its cap.
WP-2045 made the last declared stage converge, and the job finished again on 25 October. Two
Windows-only tooling failures were a cp1252 decode of git's output and a test id over Windows'
environment limit, both fixed by WP-2043. The rest were fits with two nearby stopping points,
where rounding picks the point and so the platform picks the answer. Two of those were package
defects, each given its own WP: a non-finite step raised out of `fit` (WP-2048), and a blank frame
refined the instrument's peak terms against noise (WP-2049). Brucite's correlation count moved
with rounding (WP-2050). The others were tests asking a question rounding answers. A LaB6 fit
reports its two axial ratios either way round, so the start rows order the pair. A magnetic trial
crawls for as many continuations as rounding picks, so the 150 K test no longer asserts it
converges. The crawl went to WP-1334. The `full` job's limit rose from 150 to 300 minutes, and the
Windows job's from 45 to 120, each from the measured job times. Each rounding failure was reproduced
on a Mac by a 1e-14 relative nudge to one start value before its fix and checked after. Both nightlies
of 4 November passed all six jobs (runs 41837816600 and 41874118202; `full` 14 839 passed, 0
failed, in 2:47:18 on the second). PRs #1146, #1165, #1309.
