## How v1.13 is getting here

### 1.12.0 cut and published (closed 2026-11-18)

The sixth weekly cut came fourteen days after 1.11.0 and carried 150 merged pull requests.
The staged notes were far from complete. Of the pull requests that changed source and did not
touch the notes, 61 had no mention, and the cut wrote a note for each one a user can see.

Two staged claims did not hold. The notes said a 1.12 result opens in 1.11.0. Measured both
ways against 1.11.0's PyPI wheel, 1.11.0 refuses every structure, result and project 1.12
writes, and opens its instrument. The notes also gave the kernel floor as 1.4, where the pin
had been 1.5 since #1554. The maintainer ruled that the rebuilt GUI at `/next` is announced,
with its unfinished state stated (2026-11-18).

The nightly dispatched on the release commit passed its three gate jobs. `full` failed on the
swapped minimum WP-1418 tracks, and the macOS job got no runner. Neither gates. The counts are
in [v1.12's appendix](v1.12.md#appendix--acceptance-at-ship-2026-11-18). The version and the
notes merged in #1687.

### A stage report costs a third less, and says what it cost (1335, closed 2026-11-03)

Issues #245, #250 and #251 measured a 4 s fit taking 112 s to deliver, all of the difference in
the report. A `summary()` and a `report()` now build one report between them (`summary(report=)`,
PR #259). A stage report costs about a third less on NAC, whose seven stage reports went from
7.1-8.3× the bare fit to 4.7-5.7× (CPU seconds on a loaded machine), and about a sixth less on the
49 000-point LaB6+cBN fit, the shape #250 reported. Every report field was byte-identical on both
fits, the two where it was checked. Its cost is countable rather than inferred:
`fit_end.stage_report_seconds` carries the rungs' wall time, a key on an existing kind, so no
`EVENT_SCHEMA_VERSION` bump. What remains is
the Stephens strain arm's Gauss-Newton solves and the March-Dollase grid, and cutting either
would make a rung less accurate than the final report, so the cost stopped there by ruling. A
phase with no free structural parameter is still analysed, since a held width model is where a
directional misfit has nowhere else to show. A joint fit still declines `stage_reports=`, and
whether the one-third cut is enough to offer it went to the maintainer. PRs #1346, #1349, #1364.
