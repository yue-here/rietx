### A strain block that cannot leave its cone, tried and declined (1946, closed 🛑 2026-11-17)

The Stephens strain variance was rewritten as a sum of squares, so it could not go negative for
any hkl. Averaged over the Laue group, that form reaches each class's physical set exactly, by
Hilbert's theorem on ternary quartics, with one argument for every class. As a refinement it
fails on round-robin brucite. Both general maps carry directions that move nothing in every class
but the triclinic one, and brucite's best physical answer sits on the edge of the set, where the form loses its
derivative. Five starts 1e-14 apart spread by 1.8e-4 for the general form and 2.8e-2 for a
four-parameter trigonal one, against a 1e-9 bar and the default driver's 8.1e-4, so the default
cone stays. The search also showed that the default driver holds σ² ≥ 0 only at the fitted
reflections. Its brucite answer is negative on about 1 % of directions between them, and the
manual now says so. The probe is `examples/probe_stephens_sos.py`. A joint fit that never seeds
the block went to issue #1663. PR #1662.
