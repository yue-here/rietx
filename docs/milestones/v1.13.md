## How v1.13 is getting here

### 1.12.0 cut and published (closed 2026-11-18)

The sixth weekly cut came fourteen days after 1.11.0 and carried 150 merged pull requests.
The staged notes were far from complete. Of the pull requests that changed source and did not
touch the notes, 61 had no mention, and the cut wrote a note for each one a user can see.

Two staged claims did not hold. The notes said a 1.12 result opens in 1.11.0. Measured both
ways against 1.11.0's PyPI wheel, 1.11.0 refuses every structure, result and project 1.12
writes, and opens its instrument. The notes also gave the kernel floor as 1.4, where the pin
had been 1.5 since #1554. The maintainer ruled that the rebuilt GUI at `/next` is announced,
with its unfinished state stated (2026-11-18).

The nightly dispatched on the release commit passed its three gate jobs. `full` failed on the
swapped minimum WP-1418 tracks, and the macOS job got no runner. Neither gates. The counts are
in [v1.12's appendix](v1.12.md#appendix--acceptance-at-ship-2026-11-18). The version and the
notes merged in #1687.

### WP-1464 a screen reads the batch references first (closed 2026-11-19)

An agent about to run a screen of candidate fits is now sent to the batch references before it
launches, and those references carry what one such screen paid for (#1681). A free cell let an
absent candidate take a present phase's peaks: on the CPD zincite pattern an absent fluorite shrank
its cell 10.8 % and took 14 ± 12 wt %, too little for any code to fire. Pinning the kernel pool to
one thread per worker, which the skill had advised, made a 10-worker screen 1.9-2.8× slower, so
the advice was reversed and the package keeps its default. Then `rietx runs` (#1695, the verb
decided by the maintainer on 2026-11-19) summarises the run directories a batch leaves, one block
per label: runs by state, the wall from first start to last end, and the slowest run against the
median, failed runs timed too. The screen behind the WP had printed a `nan` median, because its
failed jobs carried no time, and never saw its 76-minute failure. Checked while a job runs, the
verb also prints how long its longest live run has gone against that median (the maintainer's
ruling of 2026-11-19), so a runaway shows before it ends.

### WP-2222 the closing stage off by default (closed 2026-11-19)

Users complained of fit time, and Yue ruled TOPAS's accuracy sufficient. A
default plan therefore runs its declared stages alone, at 1.2-1.9× less wall
clock on the measured cases, and its answers move 0.16-2.0 esd from the
closing stage's. `CLOSING_WINDOW_TOL` (1e-3) stays the opt-in for an answer to
publish. Three places keep it on Yue's ruling of the same day, each where an
error spreads beyond one fit: a Pawley series carrying intensities (its Rwp
drifted 0.034 → 0.037 over four patterns without it), the `lab_calibrate`
preset, and the guides that quote numbers. A GUI preview starting from a
default fit shifts by up to 0.13 % of the strongest peak; WP-2226 retunes it.

### A clicked value opens on its full stored value (WP-2223, closed 2026-11-19)

Yue asked how a value drawn as ×10ⁿ looks once clicked, and ruled "go with best practice". The
GUI now follows the spreadsheet model in the Parameters panel and the Interactive tab, on both
pages. A number is drawn rounded and edited to its last digit, and text left as it opened is no
edit. Before, Enter on an unchanged Interactive value moved it by its 13th digit. The drawn
digits typed into a Parameters cell are now an edit, since they are a different number from
the one the editor showed. The Interactive box reads `1.5×10⁻³` as the range fields do.
PR #1709.

### WP-2224 a re-fit of a converged model runs one stage (closed 2026-11-19)

A second `fit` on a model its last fit of the same pattern converged runs one stage,
`warm_refit`, freeing at once what the plan frees. Under WP-2222's default it lands within
0.045 esd of the first fit's answer on the eight comparison standards, at 0.15-0.27 of a staged
re-fit's evaluations. A first fit stays staged: from a cold start one stage reached 20 of 48
starts. On Yue's rulings of the same day a re-fit runs staged when its plan frees a parameter
the converged fit held, since that parameter starts cold (brucite's March-Dollase block landed
2.25 esd off in one stage), and a model swap through `edit` stays staged. A rejected one stage
falls back to the plan with `WARM_REFIT_REJECTED`. `summary()` says which way a re-fit ran.
The GUI still refines staged; WP-2227 gives it a view of how a run went.

### WP-2101 the parametric surface as it stands, in the manual (closed 2026-11-19)

Someone arriving from TOPAS or GSAS-II can now find what rietx's parametric refinement
does in the pages they read first. `switching/topas.md` maps TOPAS's parametric vocabulary
(`prm`, `local`, `#list`, an equation on a `prm`, `!`) to rietx's spellings, lists the
constructs that have no spelling yet, and gives a five-step recipe for porting an input
that does not import. `switching/gsas2.md` shows a GSAS-II sequential refinement as a
rietx chain. `reference/parametric.md` tables the accepted target kinds, with an executed
block. The WP's inventory counts every parametric construct in two real TOPAS inputs
against its rietx spelling, and the milestone's agent row is judged against it.
`guides/equation.md` documents two traps: a coefficient no stage frees, and a plan that
cannot reach a variable. On Yue's rulings of 2026-11-19 a fit now names both with
`VARIABLE_NOT_FREED`, a warning in a joint fit and in a single fit, which a `hold`
silences in a single fit. A refusal of an unknown target names `.parameters()`. PRs
#1573, #1632, #1654 and #1714; a joint fit's `hold` is WP-2103's.

### WP-2150 a tied cell value that contradicts its space group is repaired at read or refused (closed 2026-11-19)

A cell that contradicts its space group's ties no longer builds silently. Before, under a
cubic group b = 5.9 beside a = 5.641 became 5.641 with no word. Now the parameter table
refuses it, naming the entry, both values and the symbol, at a relative bound of 1e-5
(at most 10 ppm in d). A CIF whose two values agree within the digits it prints is read as
the tied cell and says so with `CIF_CELL_TIE_CORRECTED`. A project file from GSAS-II,
GSAS, TOPAS, FullProf or a recipe is refused naming the file, and `Project.create` refuses
before it writes the directory. A history saved by an older release opens with its stored
nodes repaired and `HISTORY_CELL_TIE_CORRECTED`. The cost: a script that edits
`cell.a.value` alone on a cubic or tetragonal phase now raises. Yue approved the three new
reader texts and the table's refusal on 2026-11-19. The indexing acceptance ranking is
unchanged. PR #1698, from issue #1669; the Jana reader's call is WP-1314's.

### A Stephens block is drawn over every direction (1318, closed 2026-11-16)
`rx.viz.plot_strain_surface` draws a phase's anisotropic-strain block as a surface over
reciprocal-space directions. The distance and colour are the FWHM of ΔM/M in ppm, and the values
are `crystallography.stephens.directional_sigma2`: the module's own sum read at the hkl of unit M
along each direction, so along any reflection it equals that reflection's σ²(M)·d⁴ (pinned to
1e-12 of the largest value in four Laue classes, and to 1e-10 on the refined brucite block). An
isotropic block is a sphere of its microstrain. A direction outside the physical cone is drawn red
at √|σ²|, never clipped, and the guard's `STEPHENS_STRAIN_NOT_POSITIVE` finding is printed
beneath, or a line saying the guard reported nothing for the block; the guard tests reflections
and the figure the sphere, so the two can differ. On the brucite acceptance fit under TRF half the
sphere is outside the cone. Theory section `microstructure.md` § The strain surface with its
committed figure pair. A GUI view and a confidence band propagated from the S_HKL covariance are
left to #1624. PR #1620.


### WP-2219 a parameter the data cannot place has no esd (closed 2026-11-21)

A parameter on a combination the data do not determine now reports no esd. Before, it
reported its spread along the directions the data did measure. That number was short by a
factor nothing bounds: on synthetic LaB6 with W, `gauss_size` and `gauss_strain` free, two
starts landed 140 esd apart, and the esd jumped by 10⁷ across the inversion's cut. The rule
holds in a single fit, a joint fit and a Pawley fit. `COVARIANCE_DIRECTION_DISCARDED` keeps
firing and says the parameters have no esd. The CIF writes `?` in the su item. This follows
linear-models practice (Searle 1971); GSAS-II and TOPAS quote the truncated esd. Holding W on
the width triple was built and reverted, because the full suite showed it moved answers the
WP did not name. Two calls went to WP-2230 on the maintainer's ruling: an estimable
combination such as a tie's sum loses its esd today, and 535 Pawley intensities on a
discarded direction sit in no overlap group. PR #1718.
