### A structure figure goes to a ray tracer (1537, closed 2026-11-17)

`render_structure` writes a POV-Ray 3.7 scene for a `.pov` path and a glTF
2.0 binary for a `.glb` path, beside the PNG. Each carries the figure's atoms
as exact ellipsoids with their principal rings, its sticks, polyhedra, cell,
letters, camera and light, so a ray tracer adds the shadows and ambient
occlusion the CPU renderer leaves out. POV-Ray's frame is left-handed, and the
scene draws through the view rotation and a mirror; on NAC (I2₁3) every atom,
carried through the written camera, lands on the PNG's pixel and depth, and
without the mirror it would not. The glTF nodes are rotation and scale only,
with a sheared shape baked into its own mesh. Nothing was ray-traced, since
neither program was installed; the renders, the Khronos validator run and the
GUI's export menu are issue #1667. PR #1666.
