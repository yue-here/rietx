## How v1.13 is getting here

### 1.12.0 cut and published (closed 2026-11-18)

The sixth weekly cut came fourteen days after 1.11.0 and carried 150 merged pull requests.
The staged notes were far from complete. Of the pull requests that changed source and did not
touch the notes, 61 had no mention, and the cut wrote a note for each one a user can see.

Two staged claims did not hold. The notes said a 1.12 result opens in 1.11.0. Measured both
ways against 1.11.0's PyPI wheel, 1.11.0 refuses every structure, result and project 1.12
writes, and opens its instrument. The notes also gave the kernel floor as 1.4, where the pin
had been 1.5 since #1554. The maintainer ruled that the rebuilt GUI at `/next` is announced,
with its unfinished state stated (2026-11-18).

The nightly dispatched on the release commit passed its three gate jobs. `full` failed on the
swapped minimum WP-1418 tracks, and the macOS job got no runner. Neither gates. The counts are
in [v1.12's appendix](v1.12.md#appendix--acceptance-at-ship-2026-11-18). The version and the
notes merged in #1687.

### WP-2036: main is green on every nightly leg (closed 2026-11-09)

`nightly.yml` was red every night from 2026-10-16, for four unrelated reasons that shared one
acceptance. The Linux full suite stopped finishing inside its limit once WP-2002's closing
stage landed; WP-2045 cut that cost and the job finishes again. Two Windows tooling tests
failed on a test id and a cp1252 decode, both fixed by WP-2043. The `qpa` guide's esd differed
tenfold between Linux and the Mac. The cause was a step in the Sabine Laue factor that a
forward-difference column straddled on one platform, and WP-2044 made the factor continuous.
The `moment` guide's bar stopped reproducing. Four slow rows failed by rounding between two
nearby answers. Two of them were the tests' fault: a LaB6 fit reports its two axial ratios
either way round, and a magnetic trial crawls for a number of steps rounding picks, so both
tests now ask what the data decides. The rest were package defects, each filed as its own WP
(2048, 2049, 2050) and landed. The Windows job's limit went from 45 to 120 minutes and the
`full` job's from 150 to 300, each read off the last nightlies' job times; the `full` job had
been cut off twice with the suite about 90 % done (#1309).

The first nightly after #1309 (11-03) failed one row outside this WP, the placement episode
reading `instrument.geometry.axial_hl`, which WP-2012's #1387 fixed. Both 11-04 runs then
passed all six jobs; the dispatched one's `full` job took 2 h 47 min of pytest time, inside the
new limit. The 11-05 nightly failed its `full` job on one row, the wavelength suite's
neutron-width pins, which #1391 (the extinction opt-in) had moved and #1429 re-pinned
(merged 2026-11-05 22:18Z). The first nightly on `main` after #1429, run 42225042080 on
2026-11-06 at `263227986`, passed all six jobs, and the 11-07 and 11-08 nightlies passed too.

The full suite on an arm64 Mac (darwin/arm64, `[dev]` venv, Python 3.12.13, on `078f10644`)
ended 5 failed, 14 841 passed, 250 skipped, 53 xfailed. Three of the five are cleared. One was
this branch's own WP index, stale until it was regenerated. Two pass on `main` at `541f0daf7`,
fixed by #1412. A fourth, the wavelength suite's neutron-width row, was a stale pin on every
platform and #1429 repinned it. The last, the two-site magnetic solve (m1 0.6995 against
3.5 ± 0.01), is not a Mac-only row: the Linux `full` job failed it with the same 0.6995 on
2026-11-11 (run 43026125239) and passed it on 2026-11-12 (run 43234222625) with no change to the
code between. On any platform it lands on the true assignment or the swap by rounding, as
WP-1418's 2026-11-12 entry measured, so a green `full` job on it says nothing. Issue #1424 tracks
it and #1559 (part b) changes the test to assert what the pattern measures. The close holds until
#1559 has merged and a nightly on `main` after it passes all six jobs.
