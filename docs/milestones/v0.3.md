# v0.3 — multi-phase QPA: measured acceptance record 🔶

**Status: acceptance measured and green 2026-07-24; the milestone is not yet
shipped** — WP-0308 (multi-histogram) and WP-0309 (exporters) are in the v0.3
scope and still open. This record exists now because the v0.3 *acceptance
criterion* (SRM 676a / IUCr QPA round-robin fractions, WP-0310) is committed
and measured; the ROADMAP row flips to ✅ when 0308/0309 land. Milestone
tracking: [../ROADMAP.md](../ROADMAP.md); design rationale:
[../DESIGN.md](../DESIGN.md).

Acceptance: quantitative phase analysis against the NIST SRM 676a certificate
(absolute anchor) and the IUCr CPD QPA round-robin weighed compositions
(tolerance referenced to the published participant spread — Madsen et al. 2001
J. Appl. Cryst. 34, 409; Scarlett et al. 2002 J. Appl. Cryst. 35, 383).
Committed as `tests/test_acceptance_srm676a.py` and
`tests/test_acceptance_qpa_roundrobin.py` (13 tests, `slow`, every refinement
writing obs/calc/diff PNGs to `tests/output/`); data provenance and the full
weighed-composition table in `tests/data/README.md`. Protocol (identical for
every pattern, no reference code to mirror — the target is the *weighed
composition*): scale+background → zero+displacement → cells → instrument
profile → per-phase size/strain (softplus terms seeded off the zero floor) →
Kα2 ratio + axial → Biso; coordinates held at single-crystal literature
values; Cu Kα doublet on the NIST/Hölzer wavelength scale; Chebyshev-6
background; Poisson weights (counts-only data).

**Measured acceptance — SRM 676a corundum cell anchor** (2026-07-24,
`qarr/corundum.prn`, a lab corundum *specimen* — NIST publishes no raw 676a
pattern): Rwp = 14.4 %, GoF = 1.61 over 7251 points;
a = 4.757866 Å (**−313 ppm** vs certified 4.759355(80)), c = 12.988632 Å
(**−283 ppm** vs 12.99231(15)), the two axes offset by the same relative
amount (|Δa/a − Δc/c| = 3×10⁻⁵) ⇒ a uniform d-scale systematic of this
uncalibrated instrument, while **c/a = 2.729928 lands +30 ppm** from the
certificate's 2.729846 — the axial ratio is where uniform zero/displacement/
wavelength systematics cancel, so it carries the certificate-grade assertion
(band 1×10⁻⁴; absolute axes get a lab-realistic 6×10⁻⁴). Refined
Kα2/Kα1 = 0.43 (graphite passband clipping the 0.5 emission ratio),
Biso(Al/O) = 0.23/0.22 Å², zero −0.075°, displacement +0.008 mm. The
certificate's ±17 ppm(a) absolute band is **not met and not claimed**: the
{zero, displacement, cell} triple decorrelates only when a certified cell is
*held* (the `lab_calibrate` lesson), which a cell-accuracy test cannot do, and
the specimen's provenance as SRM 676a is undocumented anyway. The certified
crystalline mass fraction 99.02 ± 1.11 % is an **amorphous** quantity
(internal-standard territory, v2 fence): `RefinementResult.qpa` reports
fractions of the modelled crystalline content — ≡ 1 here, asserted as the
convention, never as the 99.02 %.

weighed compositions (corundum/zincite/fluorite; Rwp 10.8-16.7 %, GoF
1.31-1.57):

| 1a | +0.61 | −0.57 | −0.04 |
| 1b | −0.12 | −0.02 | +0.14 |
| 1c | +1.26 | −1.72 | +0.47 |
| 1d | +1.85 | −3.87 | +2.02 |
| 1e | +2.21 | −2.32 | +0.12 |
| 1f | +3.17 | **−5.13** | +1.96 |
| 1g | +2.39 | −3.91 | +1.52 |
| 1h | +2.17 | −3.72 | +1.54 |

Worst |ΔW| = 5.1 wt % (1f zincite); every trace determination (< 5 wt %
weighed) within 1.3 wt %. Tolerance 6 wt % majors / 2 wt % trace, referenced
to the participant spread (several wt % even for this deliberately
well-behaved sample; matching the weighed values much better than that spread
would be suspicious, per the WP-0310 brief). The signed errors have a stable
**shape** — zincite low (mean −2.7), corundum high (+1.7), fluorite mildly
high (+1.0) — the direction untreated microabsorption imposes on the
corundum/zincite pair (µ ≈ 128 vs 282 cm⁻¹); fluorite's sign shows the effect
is also particle-size dependent, and no d50s are published to model it
honestly, so Brindley is deliberately **not** applied to sample 1 (the round
robin's own finding: participants who corrected the well-behaved sample often
made it worse). The shape is asserted as a test
(`test_sample1_bias_has_the_microabsorption_shape`) so a change that breaks —
or fixes — the physics fails loudly and prompts re-derivation. The propagated
σ(W) (0.1-0.4 wt %, correlated scale block) understates the real error by an
order of magnitude: QPA accuracy is systematics-dominated, and the tolerances
never lean on σ(W).

**Measured acceptance — sample 2 (+ 36 wt % platy brucite)**: Rwp = 13.2 %,
GoF = 1.54; errors +0.98 / −2.91 / −0.02 / **+1.95 (brucite)** wt % — worst
2.9, inside the same 6 wt % band despite severe texture. March-Dollase on
brucite's (001) plate normal refines to **r = 0.670** (< 1 = platy in
reflection geometry, the physically expected habit; the pure brucite pattern
fits at Rwp 55 % without the correction, 19 % with it — WP-0307's machinery
doing real work on real data). H stays in the model for the 3.5 % of molar
mass it carries, with its Biso held (X-rays cannot see it; freed, it pins at
the 25 Å² bound).

**Measured acceptance — sample 4 (microabsorption, designed to defeat
Brindley)**: Rwp = 27.9 %, GoF = 2.64 (coarse-grained specimen — granular
residuals throughout). Uncorrected fractions: corundum **+24.2**, magnetite
**−15.1**, zircon **−9.2** wt % — the microabsorption shape (Fe sits just
above its K edge at Cu Kα, µ(Fe₃O₄) ≈ 1165 cm⁻¹ vs corundum's 128).
Brindley with order-of-magnitude radii (0.5/5/1.5 µm; no d50s published)
moves corundum to +20.6 and magnetite to −11.2 but zircon slightly *away*
(−9.4), and the **µR fence fires** on magnetite (µR = 0.57) and zircon
(0.057) — the WP-0305 machinery correctly refusing confidence outside its
|µ−µ̄|R regime rather than silently "correcting". **No accuracy band is
claimed**: the test asserts the bias directions, the fence, and that the
correction improves the two extreme phases — Scarlett et al.'s own verdict
("really beyond the limits of the Brindley model in this case") reproduced,
characterised, and pinned by assertions instead of absorbed into a tolerance.

Engineering notes:
- No core code changes were needed — the entire WP is tests + records. The
  CPD `.prn` format turned out to be plain two-column ASCII already covered
  by the generic xy reader (locked in by a fast reader test; the 0310
  handover's "needs a new reader" was wrong). Structure models for all six
  phases live in the test module with literature citations; gemmi resolves
  bare `F d -3 m` / `I 41/a m d` to **origin choice 1**, so magnetite and
  zircon carry explicit `:2` symbols.
- Full suite is now ~2 min (was ~21 s) — the ten 7251-point Rietveld
  refinements dominate; the `-m "not slow"` path (313 tests) stays fast.
