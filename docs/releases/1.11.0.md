> **Staging file, not yet a release.** Opened 2026-10-24, the day 1.10.0 was
> cut, so that a break or a user-facing addition is recorded the day it lands
> (ROADMAP protocol rule 6). There is no `1.11.0` on PyPI and no `v1.11.0` tag
> until `RELEASING.md` is walked. The cut writes the framing, and checks this
> file against the tag range `v1.10.0..main`. Stage each note under the

## Upgrading

Code written against 1.10.0 runs unaltered, with the exceptions in this list.

### Code to check

- `capabilities().schema_version` is `0.60`, where 1.10.0's was `0.59`.
  0.60 adds `RefinementResult.radiation` and `HistogramResult.radiation` (WP-2042), and `IndexingResult.formula`, `IndexingResult.temperature` and `PeakList.instrument_undeclared` (WP-2030).
  0.60 adds `RefinementResult.radiation` and `HistogramResult.radiation` (WP-2042), and `Phase.distortion_modes` (WP-1419).
  A model or result carrying `Phase.distortion_modes` does not open in 1.10.0.
  0.60 adds `RefinementResult.radiation` and `HistogramResult.radiation` (WP-2042), and `PlanarityRestraint` in the restraint union with `"planarity"` in `RestraintRow.kind` (WP-1809).
  A model or result carrying either planarity name does not open in 1.10.0.
- `predicted_but_absent` is a **capping** caveat (C3, issue #916, PR #941): a cell carrying it alone grades `medium`, where 1.10.0 graded it `low`; `INDEX_REFUTING_CAVEATS` no longer names it.
  Code that read the caveat as a refutation reads `supercell_refuted` instead, which is what refutes an oversized cell now.
- `LeBailValidation` gains `predicted_but_absent_uncancellable`, `predicted_but_absent_uncancellable_in_window` and `absent_source`; `SupercellCheck` gains `relation`, and its `index` is 0 when the parent is not a sublattice (schema 0.60, indexing thresholds 1.12).
  Every field is defaulted to the reading a 1.10.0 document had.
- The indexer validates each candidate by a Pawley fit, where 1.10.0 ran one Le Bail pass (WP-2011).
  `LeBailValidation.mode` records which (schema 0.60), and a 1.10.0 document reads `"lebail"`.
  The type, `CellCandidate.lebail`, `validate_by_lebail` and `lebail_rwp` keep their names.
- Under a time ceiling (`quick`, or a declared `total_budget_seconds`) the search stops 15 % of it early, where 1.10.0 stopped 8 % early, and the validation fits run cheapest first, each on what the clock holds, where 1.10.0 gave each an equal share in rank order (WP-2011).
  `rietx.indexing.engines.budget_exhausted_diagnostic` loses its `ceiling_hit` argument, since a validation fit no longer has a share of its own to run out of.
  Code that compared two candidates' `lebail.rwp` reads a different number: a cell that predicts every reflection of another now fits at least as well, so the comparison separates a wrong metric and never an oversized cell.
- `determine_extinction_symbol` now refines the cell before it screens (`refine_cell=True` is the default, issue #726).
  A caller that hands it a certified cell and wants that cell held passes `refine_cell=False`.
  A caller that reads `best_or_none()` gets `None` on a new reason as well: `ExtinctionScreen.status == "unreliable"`.
  0.60 also adds `ExtinctionScreen.prefit_rwp` and `ExtinctionScreen.reference_rwp_net`, each defaulting to `None`.
- A chemical formula whose full stop reads two ways is refused (WP-2030): `formula_unit_volume`, `SearchSpec.from_formula` and `index_pattern(formula=)` raise `ValueError` on `"CuSO4.5H2O"`, which 1.10.0 read as 4.5 O, 39 % low.
  What is refused is a decimal count of 1 or more, with no leading zero in its fractional part, straight after an element or a bracket and followed, past any whitespace, by one of 13 adduct molecules (H2O, D2O, NH3, H2O2, HCl, CO2, CH3OH, CH4O, C2H5OH, C2H6O, CH3CN, C6H6, C4H8O) that ends the part: `"CuSO4.5H2O"`, `"CuSO4.5 H2O"`, `"CuSO4.5 H2 O"`.
  Write a hydrate with `·` (`"CuSO4·5H2O"`).
  Every other decimal reads as 1.10.0 read it: `"Fe2.95O4"`, `"Li1.05Mn1.95O4"`, `"Ca9.5(PO4)6F2"` and `"Mg0.5SiO3"` are unchanged.
  A solvate whose molecule is not on the list (`"ZnCl2.2C5H5N"`) still reads as a decimal.
- A plan's last declared stage now converges at the solver's 1e-9 when a closing stage follows it, as it did before 1.10.0 (WP-2045).
  `RefinementPlan.stage_ftols()` ends `[..., None, None]` where it ended `[..., 1e-6, None]`, and that stage's `StageResult.ftol` reads `None`.
  A stage's own `Stage.ftol` still wins.
- Code that filters a joint result's `BISO_NEGATIVE`, `BISO_UNUSUALLY_LARGE` or `ADP_NOT_POSITIVE_DEFINITE` findings by the bare path of a per-histogram parameter (WP-2028).
  Such a finding's path now reads `hist.<h>.…`, where 1.10.0 wrote it bare from pattern 0.
- `SEQUENTIAL_DISCONTINUITY` is gone, with its three thresholds `DISCONTINUITY_FACTOR`, `DISCONTINUITY_SIGMA` and `MIN_POINTS_FOR_DISCONTINUITY` (WP-2003).
  A series no longer calls any step a jump: what one means depends on the sample's history, which no pattern records.
  `SeriesResult.discontinuities` is renamed `SeriesResult.steps`, and it now lists every refined path's step between consecutive patterns, in esds of the later fit (`SeriesStep.size_esd`), largest first, rather than the one flagged step per path.
  The summary's trajectory block names the largest; `rx.explain("SeriesResult.steps")` carries the reading.
  `fit(verify_discontinuities=True)` is `fit(verify_steps=k)`, which refits the k largest steps cold and writes `SeriesStep.cold_ratio`, where the diagnostic's `value` held it; a bool is refused.
  A series saved by 1.10.0 opens with its `discontinuities` dropped and `steps=None`.
- A rigid body whose atoms straddle a symmetry element is now refused, `RIGID_BODY_IMAGE_OVERLAP` (WP-1807, #876).
  In 1.10.0 such a body built and fitted in silence while the cell held part of it twice: a PbSO₄ SO₄ moved 0.03 off its mirror counted S 8 and O 32 in a cell of 4 and 16.
  A model or `.rex` history holding one now refuses at the next table build, naming the pair of atoms, the operation, the distance and the doubled counts.
  A body whose atoms carry a `disorder_group`, or whose overlapping atoms and images in one place have occupancies summing to 1 or less, is not refused.
  The remedy is in the message: put the body's origin on the element and list one atom per image.

### Numbers that move

- A fit whose last declared stage ran away no longer stalls in its closing stage (WP-2045).
  At 1e-6 that stage could stop on a plateau of its own frozen peak windows, where a widening axial ratio pushed intensity out of the model.
  On the IUCr round-robin series carried whole from mixture to mixture, cpd-1b's warm fit stopped there with `axial_sl` at 557.8, and its closing stage ran for tens of minutes.
  It now stays on its warm attempt at `axial_sl` 0.008 and Rwp 0.140, against 0.137 for the fit from the initial model, and the two patterns take 8 s on an arm64 Mac.
  `closing_window_tol=None` is no longer needed for that series.
  On the four acceptance protocols the change costs 1.04-1.07 times the evaluations and moves no answer by 0.001 esd.
  A series can now spend one more fit after a real jump: on the path guide's planted 1.5 % cell step, the warm start after the step fails and the pattern is fitted from the starting model, reaching the same Rwp of 0.040.
- A joint fit's curves and statistics come from a compile at its answer, as a single fit's do (WP-1421).
  Under a default plan the closing stage already ends there, and the WP-1131 size pair reports the same five digits.
  With `closing_window_tol=None`, a pattern whose last stage widened its peaks moves: Rwp 0.09658 → 0.10207 on that pair, whose report stops listing 38 cut peak tails as unmatched peaks.
- The indexing pipeline's reversed questions are asked at the measured resolution by a candidate-independent line screen (`rietx.indexing.screen`, C3, #941), not of the candidate's own Le Bail fit and not in the search window.
  `predicted_but_absent` counts the lines the lattice predicts where the picker's background envelope shows nothing, at the peak list's widths, so a wrong cell can no longer buy a count of zero by refitting its background: on the IUCr round-robin magnetite pattern the primitive description of the correct F lattice read 0 of 163 absent in 1.10.0 and reads its absences now, and the correct cell's d-glide absences are counted but, being cancellable, never reach the caveat.
  The caveat fires on the **windowed** uncancellable absences (inside the first twenty observed lines), where every true cell measured reads 0–2 and every wrong one 7–79 (windowed counts, tabulated in #941's description).
  `predicted_seen_fraction` is the screen's seen fraction at one FWHM after the candidate's common shift: on the synthetic Si pattern of issue #916 the trigonal description that outranked the truth falls from 0.77 to 0.34 against the truth's 0.73.
  `supercell_checks` asks child against parent whenever the parent's indexed lines are a subset of the child's and the child's line set is the denser, so cubic I √2·a₀ over cubic F a₀ (volume ratio 5.66) and cubic P 2a₀ (32) are refuted, 0 of 12 and 0 of 85 extras seen; `refuted` now needs the extras seen no more often than chance, so SRM 676a's cell as a child of its c/2 subcell (8 of 18 at p₀ 0.21) reads `undecided` rather than `refuted`.
  `validate_by_lebail(freeze_profile=True)` fits every candidate with one shared profile for a caller who wants that.
- The indexer's validation fit is a Pawley fit (WP-2011), so every candidate's `lebail.rwp` and `unmatched_observed` move.
  One Le Bail pass re-extracted the intensities only before its solve, and on true cells it stalled: on the round-robin corundum and zircon patterns it left the Gaussian width `w` on its lower bound and 21 and 44 observed peaks unmatched, where the Pawley fit hits no bound and leaves 12 and 11.
  `INDEX_IMPURITY_LINES` fires less often on a true cell for that reason, and the GUI's candidate row shows a lower Rwp.
  A cell whose lattice contains the truth's now reads about the truth's Rwp: magnetite's primitive description 0.174 against the true F cell's 0.180, where one Le Bail pass read 0.65 against 0.21.
  The absent count is the line screen's and does not move.
  Without a peak list the absent count still reads the fit's background, which no longer sinks under a wrong cell: LaB6 with its cell 1 % long reads 14 of 28 absent, where it read 0.
- The extinction screen's answers move under its new default, a cell-free `profile_only` Le Bail of the lattice group before the screen (#726).
  The prefit starts from a seeded background when the instrument's is the preset's all-zero one.
  Every pinned class is unchanged and its ΔBIC moves: certified SRM 676a corundum keeps `R - c -` at −1301 where it was −3434, and the FAP tutorial keeps `P 63 - -` at −83 where it was −16.
  On the X-ray histogram of `tests/data/gsas2_pbso4.gpx`, at an index cell 0.1-0.2 % long, the screen went from abstaining to `P c - n`.
  The screen also gates its reference fit now.
  When that fit leaves more than half of the net signal's weighted power unexplained, the screen ranks the classes but abstains, with `EXTINCTION_SCREEN_UNRELIABLE`.
  The net is taken against the peak picker's background envelope, so no fit can move it.
  On a synthetic Si pattern at synchrotron resolution with the cell 0.4 % off and held, the screen ranked a wrong class first with confidence, and it now abstains.
  `ExtinctionScreen.prefit_rwp` and `.reference_rwp_net` record both fits, and `INDEXING_THRESHOLDS_VERSION` is 1.12, the bump C3 (#941) takes for this release.

## New

- `result.plot()` names the radiation on its 2θ axis, as `data.plot()` does, from the new `RefinementResult.radiation` record (WP-2042).
  A joint fit records each pattern's own, and a result saved earlier draws the bare axis.
- `pick_peaks` names every bound its group fit ends on, and the value (#934 (c)).
  The new diagnostic `PEAK_POSITION_AT_BOUND` lists the components whose position ran into its bound, half the group's seed FWHM from the seed, each with the bound in degrees and its I/σ_I; it is `info` everywhere, and its `value` counts those still usable above the false-discovery bar.
  The new info diagnostic `PEAK_NO_INTENSITY` lists the `no_intensity` components, excluded from `usable()` and kept in peaks, and says which sat on the zero-intensity bound, which under 1σ of their own esd and which were marked by hand.
  `PEAK_WIDTH_CAPPED` now gives each component's FWHM and the width bound, and `PEAK_EXPECTED_FALSE_LINES` each `weak_line` component's I/σ_I and the bar.
  No flag, threshold or `usable()` moves. Over the 16 IUCr round-robin lab patterns 87 components sit on their position bound, and 24 of them are usable and above the bar.
  The peak editor passes its own windows (`PeakDoc.groups`) to `peak_diagnostics`, which now also takes a mapping keyed by group number, so a bound printed after an edit is the bound of the window the component was fitted in; and a `no_intensity` flag set by hand on a line the data can tell from zero is listed as marked by hand, not as `I/σ_I … < 1`.
  `INDEXING_THRESHOLDS_VERSION` is 1.12, the bump C3 (#941) takes for this release.
- `read_pattern` reads a pdCIF whose only intensity column is `_pd_proc_intensity_net`, as in a deposit TOPAS-Academic wrote (WP-1806, #561).
  1.10.0 refused such a file as having no pattern block. A file that also states a total column reads that column, as before.
  The info diagnostic `PATTERN_INTENSITY_NET` and `PatternData.metadata["intensity_item"]` say the net column was read, since the item is defined as background-subtracted.
  `PATTERN_NET_HAS_BACKGROUND` (warning) fires when the column never comes within 5σ of zero, so either a background was left in or the range holds no empty baseline; the intensities are not changed.
  On the acridine form IX deposit (COD 2242872) it fires at 14.1σ, and σ comes from the file's weights, which carry a counting time that varies fivefold across the scan.

- A pdCIF `_pd_proc_ls_weight` of zero now excludes its point and only that point, as the pdCIF dictionary defines it ("a data point not used for refinement").
  1.10.0 dropped the whole weight column to the Poisson fallback on a single zero, with no diagnostic, so every other point lost its stated weight.
  Each run of zero-weight points becomes an `excluded_regions` entry, and the info diagnostic `PATTERN_ZERO_WEIGHT_EXCLUDED` counts them. A column with no positive weight at all is still read as no weights.
- `read_pattern` reads a Bruker DIFFRAC `.raw` version 2 (`RAW2`) file holding one range (WP-2059, issue #1164).
  1.10.0 refused every version 2 file. The data offset is read from the block header's stated length, and a file whose header and counts do not account for its size is refused, as is one declaring more than one range.
  The counts are the file's, zeros included, and σ is decided as for versions 3 and 4.
  The reader was checked against a real public version 2 file, the PbSO₄ round-robin X-ray pattern, whose 6001 counts it reads as TOPAS reads them.
  Each run of zero-weight points becomes an `excluded_regions` entry, and the info diagnostic `PATTERN_ZERO_WEIGHT_EXCLUDED` counts them. A weight column holding a negative entry, a null (`?` or `.`) or no positive entry is still read as no weights, whole, and the info diagnostic `PATTERN_WEIGHT_UNUSABLE` now says so, counting negative and missing entries separately (1.10.0 fell back to the Poisson σ without a word).
- `PHASE_HELD_IN_PATTERNS` (info) names a phase whose per-pattern structure or scale a joint fit held in the patterns that cannot see it, with how many (WP-2028).
- A rigid body can sit on a special position (WP-1807, #561).
  Its origin refines on its site's basis, and its rotation only about the axial directions the site symmetry fixes: three on a centre, one on a mirror or a 2-fold, none on a site with two or more symmetry axes.
  `body_from_atoms(..., origin=...)` takes an atom label or a point for the origin, so it can sit on the element the atoms share, and `add_body` on a special position checks the whole template maps onto itself and keeps one atom per image, warning about those it drops.
  The template check tolerates `√3 × 10⁻³` Å, the most a template typed to three decimals in Å can miss by.
  A body with an atom on an element its origin is not on, a torsion moving an on-site atom, or a site body in rhombohedral axes is refused, `RIGID_BODY_NOT_INVARIANT`, naming the atom.
  1.10.0 refused every body whose origin sat on a special position, and advised a move that doubled the composition.
  `crystallography.wyckoff.axial_basis` gives the axial directions, and `SiteOrbit.stabilizer_ops` the stabiliser as operation indices.

- A planarity restraint holds four or more atoms near their least-squares plane (WP-1809, schema 0.60).
  `PlanarityRestraint` gives one row per atom, its signed distance to the plane over σ, so the rows' sum of squares is the smallest eigenvalue of the atoms' scatter matrix (Schomaker et al. 1959), with the exact eigenvector derivative; TOPAS's `Flatten` restrains the same geometry.
  An atom may enter twice under different images, a ring across an inversion centre; a set on a line has no plane and is refused when the model compiles.
  A plane the data hold out of flat raises one `RESTRAINT_TENSION`, naming the atoms past three σ, worst first.
- A fit or a single stage whose phase carries anti-bump rows searches it again at the end (WP-1809).
  The pair list is built once, by the caller, so a pair that moved inside its minimum from outside the list has no row; the warning `RESTRAINT_PAIRS_STALE` names each such pair with its distance and the minimum the list states for its species pair. A contact already inside its minimum where the run started, such as a bond the caller left out of the list, is not named.
  A run that moves no coordinate and no cell parameter makes no search.
  The warning reads as a warning for the `qpa`, `structure` and `magnetic` purposes, whose numbers depend on atom coordinates, and as information for the others.

## Magnetic structures

- A distorted child can be refined on its mode amplitudes (WP-1419).
  `displacive_statement(parent, child, (P, p))` states the child as its parent plus one absolute amplitude, in Å, per symmetry-adapted mode, and `Phase.distortion_modes` carries them; the fit refines the amplitudes and the coordinates follow.
  A child stated at the parent is seeded off it along a direction whose stabiliser is exactly the child's group, since every amplitude outside the identity irrep has a zero column there.
  The GSAS, GSAS-II, FullProf and TOPAS project writers refuse a phase carrying `distortion_modes` with the one line that writes plain coordinates.

- `Structure.from_cif` reads a magCIF that names its parent space group under `_parent_space_group.name_H-M`, as FullProf writes it (WP-1918, issue #1063 item 2).
  1.10.0 refused such a file as stating no space group; the magnetic dictionary defines only `_parent_space_group.name_H-M_alt`, and the undefined tag can mean nothing else.
  The new info diagnostic `CIF_MAGNETIC_PARENT_TAG_UNDEFINED` names the tag that was read. A file stating both tags with different groups is refused, quoting both.

- `Structure.from_cif` reads a CIF whose structure block sits beside blocks that state nothing, no tags or only `_audit_` tags, such as an empty `data_global` ahead of it (WP-1918, issue #1063 item 1).
  1.10.0 refused such a file as "not a CIF"; the new info diagnostic `CIF_EMPTY_BLOCK_SKIPPED` names each block skipped.
  A file with two blocks holding atom sites, or one beside a block that states anything else, is still refused, and the message now names the blocks and what each states.

- A CIF using the pre-2016 draft magnetic tags (`_magnetic_atom_site_*`, `_magnetic_cell_*`, `_magnetic_space_group_symop_operation_*`) is refused by name (WP-1918, issue #1063 item 3).
  1.10.0 said "no atom sites found" where the sites were under the draft tags, and read the nuclear half of a file whose sites were under `_atom_site_`, dropping its moments with nothing said.

## Changed

- `_atom_type_scat_source` credits the `O2-` X-ray form factor to "W&K, Rez et al. (1994)", not to Waasmaier & Kirfel (1995), whose README credits that row to Rez et al. (#1039).
  The scattering docstring and `ATTRIBUTION.md` say the same; no coefficient and no computed number moves.
- `distortion_candidates` (new in 1.10.0) returns a candidate whose child it refuses beside the others, where 1.10.0 raised `ValueError` for the whole call (WP-1419, the follow-ups of #996's review).
  Code that caught the `ValueError` now receives that candidate with `child`, `transform` and `basis` `None` and `refusal` set to a `DistortionRefusal` (the code, the message, each draw's operation count); `driving` and `rest` raise the refusal's message.
  A draw that keeps fewer operations than the isotropy group is a miss of the probe's tolerance and reads `DISTORTION_CHILD_SMALLER`; `DISTORTION_CHILD_LARGER`, which says the atoms cannot realise the group, now needs every draw spglib identified to keep more.
  The code is chosen from every draw, not the last: a draw keeping extra operations followed by draws spglib could not identify reads `DISTORTION_CHILD_LARGER`, not `DISTORTION_CHILD_UNIDENTIFIED`.
- `reduce_cell` builds the reduced cell from the input's metric under the change of basis it records, on every path (WP-2035).
  1.10.0 kept gemmi's reduced vector wherever its signs agreed with that image, and on a cell within ε of a right angle a value could still sit up to about 6e-7 of the metric's scale off it; such a cell now moves by that much.
- `INDEX_DATA_INSUFFICIENT` names an undeclared instrument first only when the declared source is a laboratory tube, a Kα doublet or one line at an anode's Kα1, Kα2 or weighted mean (WP-2030).
  A correctly declared synchrotron instrument has zero axial apertures and a narrow profile too, and 1.10.0 named those as missing.
  A picked list now records what its instrument left at default (`PeakList.instrument_undeclared`), so `index_pattern(peaks)` with no instrument names it too.
  "A laboratory tube" is read to within 5e-4 Å of an anode's Kα1, Kα2 or weighted mean (2λ₁ + λ₂)/3, so `1.540593`, `1.5405` and a vendor header's `1.5418` read as Cu as `1.5405929` does.
  The field is `None` when nothing was checked (a source that is not a lab tube), and `{}` only when it was checked and everything was declared.
- `IndexingResult.formula` and `IndexingResult.temperature` record what the `INDEX_Z_NOT_INTEGER` check read, and `rietx index` takes `--formula` and `--temperature` (WP-2030).
- `Refinement.report()`, `reflection_table()`, `predict()` with no grid and `write_cif()` read the answer saved with the result (WP-1421).
  A later fit that is cancelled keeps its completed stages, and these no longer mix its values into the previous result.
- `FROZEN_COMPILE_STALE` no longer says the fit is unbiased, and says how to re-solve (WP-1421).
  A joint fit can now carry it, naming the pattern whose gap is largest.
- A site within 5e-4 (fractional, per coordinate) of a special position is counted once, on the special position they collapse to, however its phase was built (#1007).
  In 1.10.0 only `Structure.from_cif` merged such a site (`CIF_SITE_TWINS_MERGED`, #765). A project file, an edited document or a structure built in code kept it general, so PbSO₄'s S2 moved 1e-4 off the Pnma mirror put 8 S in a cell of 4, and nothing said so.
  One gate at every boundary, readers included: a site is merged only where the occupancy at its position, summed over the atoms sharing it and each × (images before / after), is above 1, since the site as stored then puts more than one atom at one place. The project readers (`to_structure`), `RefinementTree.load`, the `Refinement` verbs that take a structure in (construction, `edit`, `set_values`, `checkout`), the CIF reader and `phase_zmv` all read it. A merge says `SITE_TWINS_MERGED` (warning; `CIF_SITE_TWINS_MERGED` on a CIF read); where the product is 1 or below the numbers read as one atom off its position or as a split pair (#1088), so the site is left as stored and `SITE_TWINS_KEPT` (warning) gives both readings. **Files that now open with the site as stored:** a CIF whose site, at occupancy ≤ ½ on a mirror (or the matching fraction elsewhere), sits within 5e-4 of the position and which 1.10.0's CIF reader merged (a project file was never merged before, so none of those changes here). A merged site's coordinates the position fixes lose `vary`, and its anisotropic tensor is projected onto the allowed ones. `Phase` validation stays strict.
  ZMV, every weight fraction and the forward model move on such a phase; a split site further than that from every special position does not. The reach is 5e-4 per coordinate, where 1.10.0's CIF merge used 0.02 Å between images, so a CIF stating `y = 0.251` on a mirror site (1e-3 from ¼, past the reach) is no longer merged. The reach bounds a site's offset from its position, and the two images on a mirror sit twice that apart. Holds, user ties and free paths on coordinate DOFs follow a site's DOF renumbering on every route that moves it, or are dropped and named (`SITE_TWINS_MERGED`, or `SITE_DOFS_RENUMBERED` where nothing merged).
- A joint fit's structure guards read every pattern (WP-2028).
  In 1.10.0 `BISO_NEGATIVE`, `BISO_UNUSUALLY_LARGE` and `ADP_NOT_POSITIVE_DEFINITE` read pattern 0 alone, so a bad value in another pattern's own copy of a per-pattern path fired nothing.
  A per-pattern path is now named `hist.<h>.` in each pattern that holds the value, and a shared path is named once, bare, as before.
  `OCCUPANCY_ABOVE_ONE` reads every pattern the same way.
- A joint fit's `CELL_RUNAWAY` names a cell that a free `vars.` coefficient drives out of its window, with the coefficient, as a single fit's does (WP-2028).
- `RefinementResult.for_histogram(h)` keeps the pattern's `tick_hkl` and `phase_agreement`, and its `diagnostics` add the top-level findings that name pattern h and the fit-wide ones (WP-2028).
  A joint result's `tick_hkl` mirrors pattern 0's, beside its `ticks`.
- The report's satellite arm (`FitReport.satellites`) now enumerates over the window the model generates its own reflections over (WP-2034).
  In 1.10.0 it kept its own copy, with no margin, a 179° cap and the low cutoff in the wrong emission line's frame.
  So on a Kα1/Kα2 doublet, a satellite or lattice point whose Kα2 image alone lies inside the fitted range was left out.
  A residual peak on that image was then indexed by no candidate.
  Now a candidate's `n_satellites` and `matched` can rise at the low edge of the range, and never fall.
  No reflection list of the model itself moves.
