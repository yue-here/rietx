- **`Refinement.fit` refines one neutron time-of-flight bank** (#1548, issue #193, T-2/T-3).
  1.12.0 read a bank and refined none.
  A bank is one histogram on a flight-time axis: positions come from `instrument.source.difc`, `difa`, `tzero` and `difb`, and the peak shape is a back-to-back exponential pulse convoluted with a Gaussian (GSAS type 1) or a pseudo-Voigt (type 3), set by `instrument.source.profile_tof.*`.
  The incident spectrum (the `ITYP` functions), the d⁴·sin θ Lorentz factor and the channel width act per channel, and Rouse absorption and Sabine extinction per reflection at its own wavelength.
  A phase's size and microstrain refine through γ(d) and σ²(d).
  `fit`, `run_stage`, `predict`, `suggest`, replay and the refinement CIF reach a bank, and the result carries `result.axis == "tof"`: `result.plot(x_axis="tof")`, the report and the CIF profile use the flight-time axis, and a bank's diagnostics have their own page.
  The calibration is read (`rx.read_gsas_tof_iparm`, `rx.read_gsas2_instprm`) or stated (`rx.Instrument.tof_neutron_bank`) and comes back held; free `instrument.source.profile_tof.*` and `instrument.source.tzero` rather than a constant-wavelength row.
- **A bank's intensity basis is declared, not guessed** (#1548).
  `PatternData.intensity_basis` is `"counts"`, `"density"` or `None`, and the readers set it only from what the file says.
  A bank left at `None` is refused: `fit`, `run_stage`, `predict`, `suggest`, `cherry_pick` and `replay` raise a `ValueError` naming the argument before any work, because the two readings differ by the channel width W(T), a slope across the bank that the displacement parameters absorb (a counts bank fitted as a density put Biso more than 0.1 Å² off a true 0.5).
  Set it by hand when the reader could not: `"counts"` if the export multiplied by the bin width, `"density"` if a channel holds counts per µs. A reader that finds no declaration still warns `PATTERN_INTENSITY_BASIS_UNKNOWN`.
- **Every declared stage but the last sizes the Lorentzian window coarser** (#1548).
  `RefinementPlan.intermediate_lorentz_window_tol` is `1e-2` for those stages and `1e-4` for the last and the closing one; `StageResult.lorentz_window_tol` records what each ran at.
  On three seeds every final parameter stays within 0.0058 esd of an all-`1e-4` plan, and the Gaussian branch is bit-identical.
- **TOPAS `.inp` time-of-flight banks** read into `TOFSource` and `ProfileTOF` and write back; an unweighted peak stack is read as the pulse and a weighted one is refused.
  A coefficient the file refines through a macro's arguments, such as `TOF_PV`'s width, is built held, and `to_tof_refinement(..., diagnostics=)` names each as `TOPAS_TOF_FLAG_HELD`.
- **A bank refuses, by name,** Le Bail and Pawley extraction, several banks in one fit (`MultiHistogramRefinement`), `refine_sequential`, March-Dollase, Stephens, a spherical-harmonic texture, scalar `mu_r` and `mu_t`, restraints, a peak equation, a sharp extra peak, a magnetic moment, an all-zero `ProfileTOF`, and a σ²(d) or γ(d) polynomial that goes negative over the fitted range.
  Several banks in one fit and magnetic scattering on a bank come as their own changes.
