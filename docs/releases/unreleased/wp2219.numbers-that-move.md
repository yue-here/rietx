- A parameter on a combination the data do not determine has no esd (WP-2219).
  Its `RefinedParameter.stderr` is `None`, as a parameter the data never moved already was.
  So is a tie built on it and a quantity derived from it.
  `COVARIANCE_DIRECTION_DISCARDED` names these parameters, in single, joint and Pawley fits.
  Before, each kept the esd of the directions the data did measure, short by a factor nothing bounds.
  On synthetic LaB₆ with W, `gauss_size` and `gauss_strain` free, two starts landed 140 of those esds apart in W.
  Those three now have no esd.
  An unheld floating origin is another case: neither atom's coordinate along the polar axis has an esd, and `hold_floating_origin()` gives one.
