- **A result says which part of its file it fitted** (WP-2075, #1219).
  `RefinementResult.fit_range` holds the file's range and point count, the 2θ limits and excluded regions as asked and as applied, and the counts in the file and in the fitted channels.
  For each stretch the limits or an excluded region cut, `fit_range.outside` gives its share of the counts, its level and slope at the cut against the fitted median, its largest excess over the background envelope, and the reflections the model put in it or within 3 FWHM of its edge.
  Nothing reads these facts to decide anything.
  Where a cut removed channels, `summary()` prints a `fit range:` line beside `excluded:`.
  Handed no pattern, the pdCIF writer takes the measured range from the record.
  The fields are on the manual's fit range page.
