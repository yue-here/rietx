- **`displacive_statement` states the child's cell through its parent's** (WP-1419).
  The returned phase carries `Phase.parent_cell` beside `Phase.distortion_modes`, and the child's six cell parameters are derived and locked.
  `phases.*.cell.*` frees the parent's edges, and the child's Γ strains stay held until named (`phases.*.parent_cell.strain.*`).
  So `mccusker_structural`'s cell stage frees one edge on a tetragonal child of a cubic parent, where 1.11.0 freed the child's a and c.
  `phases.0.cell.*` reaches only the locked rows there, frees nothing, and the stage reports `STAGE_PATH_NOT_FREE`.
