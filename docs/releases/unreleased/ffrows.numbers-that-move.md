- **Magnetic form factors change for bare `Mo`, `Tc`, `Ru` and `Rh`, for `Pr3+` and `Pu3+` (and bare `Pr`, `Pu`), and for every row past its fitted range** (#1691, #1796, #1804). Brown's bare 4d rows were singly charged ions' curves; they are now the neutral atoms, computed for rietx (bare `Ru` ⟨j₀⟩ at s = 0.2 Å⁻¹: 0.4907 → 0.4163, so a moment refined with it comes out larger). Brown's printed `Pu3+` rows are fits to the Pu⁵⁺ curve, and `Pr3+` was Lisher & Forsyth's non-relativistic row; both are now computed Dirac-Fock tables (`Pu3+` ⟨j₀⟩(0.2): 0.6267 → 0.5741). Past the end of Brown's fitted range (s = 1.5 Å⁻¹; 1.2 for rare earths) every row now blends onto a computed free-ion tail instead of extrapolating its fit: Mn²⁺ ⟨j₀⟩ at s = 3 was 1.01. Inside the range every other row is unchanged, bit for bit.
