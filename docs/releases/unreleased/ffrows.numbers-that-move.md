- **Magnetic form factors change for the neutral 4d atoms (`Mo0`, `Tc0`, `Ru0`, `Rh0`, `Pd0`; bare `Mo` to `Pd` before), for `Pr3+` and `Pu3+`, and for every row past its fitted range** (#1691, #1796, #1804). Brown's bare 4d rows were singly charged ions' curves; they are now the neutral atoms, computed for rietx, each named in `approximation_name` with its configuration (`Ru0` ⟨j₀⟩ at s = 0.2 Å⁻¹: 0.4907 → 0.4163, so a moment refined with it comes out larger; `Pd0`, 4d⁹5s ³D: 0.5528 → 0.4965). Brown's printed `Pu3+` rows are fits to the Pu⁵⁺ curve, and `Pr3+` was Lisher & Forsyth's non-relativistic row; both are now computed Dirac-Fock tables (`Pu3+` ⟨j₀⟩(0.2): 0.6267 → 0.5741). Past the end of Brown's fitted range (s = 1.5 Å⁻¹; 1.2 for rare earths) every row now blends onto a computed free-ion tail instead of extrapolating its fit: Mn²⁺ ⟨j₀⟩ at s = 3 was 1.01. Inside the range every other row is unchanged, bit for bit.
- **Bare element labels on moments change form factor** (#1806). A bare 3d label was the neutral atom and is now the census ion: bare `Fe` → Fe³⁺, `Mn` → Mn²⁺, `Co`, `Ni`, `Cu` → 2+, `Cr` → Cr³⁺, `Ru` → Ru⁴⁺, so a moment refined on a bare label moves. Bare f labels move where the census pick differs from the old default: `Pu` Pu³⁺ → Pu⁴⁺, `Np` Np⁴⁺ → Np⁵⁺; bare `U` and `Eu` (U⁶⁺, Eu³⁺, no moment) and bare `Sc`, `Ti`, `V`, `Y`, `Zr`, `Nb`, `Mo`, `Tc`, `Rh`, `Pd` with a moment are refused. Write `"Fe0"` to keep the neutral atom.
