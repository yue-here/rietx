- **A fit that frees an FCJ axial ratio sitting on zero now returns a
  different answer.** A stage that newly frees S/L or H/L on zero (or on a
  positive lower bound) starts S/L at 0.02 and H/L at 0.01 (a start that would
  equal the other ratio's value takes the other number), and records the rows
  in `StageResult.seeded`. Before, the column at zero was nearly zero and a
  Gauss-Newton fit could end `converged` on zero with an esd of order 1
  (#1075; WP-1955). On #1075's repro under `lab_bragg_brentano`, S/L went from
  0.00008 ± 1.29 (Rwp 0.0831) to 0.04531 ± 0.00018 (Rwp 0.0630). The aperture
  columns themselves are now a second-order difference, within 5e-4 of the
  exact column at one zero aperture, so an esd moves where it was read off
  them (55× narrower on the nonzero aperture of `test_e8`). Fits that start
  both ratios positive are unchanged to under 0.02 esd on LaB6, FAP and
  cpd-1a. In a joint fit the two histograms of one instrument may end on
  opposite orders of the pair, which the pair's symmetry allows.
