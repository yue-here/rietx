- **A joint fit's identifiability carrier is filled in** (WP-2028). It holds the worst correlations and the softest modes of the joint matrix, named by the joint paths, and each pattern's declared-peak screen, keyed `hist.<h>.` like its background screen.
  In 1.12.0 those three tables were empty on a joint fit.
  `for_histogram(h)` keeps each pair and mode that touches that pattern's rows or a shared one, whole, its own rows bare (no `hist.<h>.` prefix), and leaves out one made only of other patterns' own rows, so a pattern's report does not quote another pattern's trade-off.
  A soft mode that joins a shared parameter to several patterns' own is reported in each.
  A joint report's summary can therefore gain a soft-mode sentence it did not have in 1.12.0.
