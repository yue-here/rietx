- The magCIF readers find a propagation vector by its loop's header and skip comments and a text field's opening line (#1378, follow-ups of #1284).
  A magCIF that read in 1.11.0 can now be refused: a k ≠ 0 listed before its `id` in a loop read as Γ, and now reaches the supercell refusal.
