- `rietx.crystallography.setting` gives `change_basis(phase, P, p)`, a phase described in new axes and origin, and `standard_setting(phase)`, the phase in ITA's standard setting and the standard cell of Parthé & Gelato (1984, 1985) (#1330, #1332).
  Both leave `predict()` unchanged; every transformed cell and coordinate comes back held, without esd, expression or bound, and the phase scale is rescaled by (V/V′)² with its esd cleared.
  `standard_setting` reports the change of basis and whether the structure came back as its mirror image (`StandardSetting.inverted`, for an enantiomorphic pair).
