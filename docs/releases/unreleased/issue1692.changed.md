- **Magnetic atoms sharing one position are judged by the moment per position** (#1692, ruled by the magnetic-path owner). On a mixed-occupancy site the powder measures only Σ occ·m, so the support test (same floor, same three esds) now reads |Σ occ·m| with its esd from the fit's covariance, and each atom's modulus is named as not separately determined.
  `MomentEvidence` and `MomentRow` carry `cosited_with`, `cosited_magnitude` and `cosited_magnitude_esd`, and `supported` on such a row is the position's verdict. `solve_magnetic` can now rank a class whose atoms each failed the test on their own, and `MOMENT_UNSUPPORTED` names a failing group with its numbers. Such atoms are no longer folded into a `MOMENT_PAIR_DEGENERATE` quadrature pair.
