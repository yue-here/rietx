- **Every magnetic form-factor row carries its source's fitted range, and a fit past it says so** (#1796). `form_factor.fitted_range(ion)` gives it: 1.5 Å⁻¹ for the 3d, 4d and actinide rows, 1.2 for the rare earths (Brown, ITC Vol. C § 4.4.5) and 1.3 for Pr³⁺ (Lisher & Forsyth 1971). A fit whose reflections reach past any other row's range runs as before and its result carries `MAGNETIC_FORM_FACTOR_EXTRAPOLATED` once, naming each row, how far it went and the d to end the data at. A direct `j0`/`j2` call past the range warns.
