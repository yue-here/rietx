- **Computed magnetic form factors, on s = 0-6 Å⁻¹** (#1689, #1691). Ru³⁺-Ru⁶⁺, Rh⁴⁺, Tc⁴⁺, Mo³⁺-Mo⁵⁺ and Nb⁴⁺ now have form factors (`magnetic_form_factor("Ru4+", s)` used to raise), served with the neutral Mo/Tc/Ru/Rh/Pd atoms, Pr³⁺ and Pu³⁺ from computed tables in `rietx/data/mff_computed.dat`, which says they were computed for rietx and are not peer-reviewed. Every Brown row carries a computed tail past his fitted range, and a fit that reaches it carries `MAGNETIC_FORM_FACTOR_COMPUTED_TAIL` once. `MomentEvidence.approximation` names each row's footing. The decks and scripts that regenerate the curves are in `tools/magnetic_form_factors/`.
