- **Computed magnetic form factors, on s = 0-6 Å⁻¹** (#1689, #1691). Ru³⁺-Ru⁶⁺, Rh⁴⁺, Tc⁴⁺, Mo³⁺-Mo⁵⁺ and Nb⁴⁺ now have form factors (`magnetic_form_factor("Ru4+", s)` used to raise), served with the neutral Mo0/Tc0/Ru0/Rh0/Pd0 atoms, Pr³⁺ and Pu³⁺ from computed tables in `rietx/data/mff_computed.dat`, which says they were computed for rietx and are not peer-reviewed. Every Brown row carries a computed tail past his fitted range, and a fit that reaches it records `MAGNETIC_FORM_FACTOR_COMPUTED_TAIL` once, as `info`: past Brown's range f² is 6 × 10⁻⁵ (Mn²⁺) to 7 × 10⁻³ (Nd³⁺) of its low-angle value, so nothing is owed. `MomentEvidence.approximation` names each row's footing. The decks and scripts that regenerate the curves are in `tools/magnetic_form_factors/`.
- **A bare element label on a moment is read as the element's most common oxidation state** (#1806). `Moment(ion="Fe")`, a magCIF site typed `Fe` and a `solve_magnetic` site of species `Fe` now take Fe³⁺'s form factor: the most common Fe charge in one ICSD census (Ding, Kumagai, Oba & Burton 2020, SI Table 1; `form_factor.read_bare_label`). `MAGNETIC_ION_ASSUMED` is now a **warning** on all three routes, naming the pick, its share and the alternatives; state the ion before quoting the moment. Where the common ion carries no moment (Sc³⁺, Ti⁴⁺, V⁵⁺, Y³⁺, Zr⁴⁺, Nb⁵⁺, Mo⁶⁺, Eu³⁺, U⁶⁺, Am³⁺) or has no row (Tc³⁺, Rh³⁺, Pd²⁺), a bare label on a moment is refused, naming the ions to state. The neutral atom is spelled `"Fe0"`, and `magnetic_ions()` lists it so. On an X-ray pattern a moment site's bare species keeps the neutral X-ray scattering factor, recorded as the new `info` code `MAGNETIC_SITE_CHARGE_NOT_STATED`. `CIF_MAGNETIC_ION_UNCHARGED` and `TOPAS_MOMENT_ION_UNCHARGED` are retired: a bare label never reads as the neutral atom now, and `MAGNETIC_ION_ASSUMED` says what it reads as. `form_factor.assumed_ion_for_element` and `resolve_assumed_ion` are replaced by `read_bare_label`.
