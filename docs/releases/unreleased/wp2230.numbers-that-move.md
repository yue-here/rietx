- A tie or a derived number the data determine keeps its esd although its sources have none (WP-2230).
  WP-2219 left every parameter on a direction the data do not determine without an esd, and everything built on one with it.
  A combination of such parameters can still be determined: `zero_shift = za + zb + zc` is, when no single term is.
  Such a tie now reports the esd of the combination, as do a bond length, a cell volume and a moment pair's quadrature sum built the same way.
  On synthetic LaB₆ the tied zero shift's esd is 1.0002× the esd of a fit that frees the zero shift alone.
  Each source parameter still has no esd.
  A geometry row or cell volume kept this way has no `stderr_diagonal`, because the sources' own variances are not measured.
