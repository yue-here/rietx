- **A magnetic fit says when its lowest lines are magnetic-only** (WP-2056, #1493).
  On a constant-wavelength pattern the axial asymmetry is largest at low angle. When a magnetic-only line the data sees lies below every nuclear-only one, no nuclear line in the sample checks the asymmetry there.
  Such a fit now carries the warning `LOW_ANGLE_ASYMMETRY_UNCALIBRATED`, at one level in every state and named on the moments, so it reads as a warning under `deliverable="magnetic"`. It lists each such line's position shift (the best-fit rigid shift of the drawn profile, with its counting-noise esd), the shift at the first nuclear-only line, and what a cot 2θ shape through the magnetic-only lines predicts there. It says whether the axial apertures were held at 0, refined in this fit, or set and held, and its suggestion names one check for each: refine one tied S/L = H/L and compare the lowest line's drawn and observed FWHM, η and position, adopting it where the three agree and otherwise quoting the shift as a systematic on the moment.
  `value` is the lowest line's shift in degrees. It fires in Le Bail and Pawley fits with the moments at zero too, and it moves no number. In those fits the magnetic-only line also draws on the `(magnetic)` tick row, not the nuclear one.
