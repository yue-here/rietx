- **A magnetic fit says when its lowest lines are magnetic-only** (WP-2056, #1493).
  On a constant-wavelength pattern the axial asymmetry is largest at low angle. When a magnetic-only line the data sees lies below every nuclear-only one, no nuclear line in the sample checks the asymmetry there.
  Such a fit now carries the info diagnostic `LOW_ANGLE_ASYMMETRY_UNCALIBRATED`. It lists each such line's position shift (the best-fit rigid shift of the drawn profile, with its counting-noise esd), the shift at the first nuclear-only line, and what a cot 2θ shape through the magnetic-only lines predicts there. It says whether the axial apertures were held at 0, refined in this fit, or set and held.
  `value` is the lowest line's shift in degrees. It fires in Le Bail and Pawley fits with the moments at zero too, and it moves no number.
