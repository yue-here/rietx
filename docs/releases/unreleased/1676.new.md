- **A diagnostic names the panel that shows it** (WP-1133, #1676).
  `Diagnostic.view` returns a panel of `rietx.viz.plot_for_vlm`, such as `"plot_for_vlm.residual"`, or `None`.
  Three codes name one: `MODEL_FAR_FROM_DATA` the overview, and `LOW_ANGLE_UNMODELLED` and `AIR_SCATTER_UNDECLARED` the Δ/σ panel.
  In a notebook a `see:` line under the diagnostic names the panel; `str(diagnostic)` is unchanged.
