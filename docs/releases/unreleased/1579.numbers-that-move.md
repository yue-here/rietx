- **A weak shoulder on a strong line's axial tail, pushed to its position bound, is no longer an indexing line**
  (WP-2120). It carries the new flag `tail_shoulder` and leaves `PeakList.usable()`; `axial_tail` or
  `position_at_bound` alone still leave a line in. On the 16 IUCr round-robin patterns 782 usable
  components become 774, seven of the eight 0.07-0.14° from every lattice line; SRM 1976a loses two and
  SRM 660c's 43.5° tail goes. No indexing acceptance row changes its answer. `fit_peaks` and the peak
  editor's refits never raise it on a position you named.
