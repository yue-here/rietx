- **A series records a warm fit's GoF over its seed's** (WP-2080, issue #1214). A
  collapsed warm fit can converge in a wrong minimum with no error code: on the
  issue's synthetic chain 2 of 20 noise draws kept a minor phase's c axis
  0.34 Å off at GoF 2.05. `SeriesEntry.warm_gof_over_seed` now records the first
  warm attempt's GoF over the GoF of the pattern it started from (about 2.0 on
  those draws), and `refit="stages"` is the rerun. Nothing is retried on it, so
  no fit changes.
