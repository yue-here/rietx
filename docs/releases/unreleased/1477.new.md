- **A weight fraction says what it rests on, and QPA at your values has a call** (WP-2073, #1213).
  `PhaseQuantity.support_sigma` is the phase's scale in esds from zero, the number `PHASE_UNCONSTRAINED` decides on (a joint fit's row carries its own histogram's reading, and the finding decides on the largest of them), and `PhaseQuantity.conditional_on` lists the phase's paths the answer held or left on a bound (a width on its zero floor is not one).
  The QPA table gains a `#` caveat for each phase with such a path, and `SeriesResult.qpa_trajectory` keeps a point whose pattern held the phase's structure and marks it (`QPATrajectory.support_sigma`, `conditional_on`, `unconstrained`).
  `Refinement.qpa(data, scale_cov=None)` forms the fractions at the values the refinement holds, with no fit, and raises `ValueError` naming the case where there is none to form; a covariance you give is propagated whole and `QuantitativePhaseAnalysis.esd_basis` says where the esds came from.
  The fields are additive and default to `None`, under schema 0.62.
