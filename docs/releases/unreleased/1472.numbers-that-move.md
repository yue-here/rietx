- **The indexing validation fit sizes its Chebyshev background from the data**
  (WP-2113, #1382). `index_pattern` and `validate_by_lebail` give the
  background the number of terms `auto_background(kind="chebyshev")` selects
  over the fitted range, and never fewer than the instrument declares. Before,
  a background the declared terms could not follow left a band in the residual,
  and Layer 0 counted each of its maxima as an observed line the cell does not
  own. On a synthetic silicon with a steep background the true cell reported 72
  `unmatched_observed` lines at Rwp 0.098 and now reports 0 at 0.029. A
  candidate's `lebail.rwp`, `gof` and `unmatched_observed` move wherever the
  declared terms were too few. No rank or grade reads those three; a fit's
  `status` and, for a call without a peak list, `predicted_but_absent` can move
  too. The new
  `LeBailValidation.background_terms` records the order, and
  `validate_by_lebail(size_background=False)` fits the declared background as
  before. Indexing thresholds 1.13.
  Schema 0.62.
