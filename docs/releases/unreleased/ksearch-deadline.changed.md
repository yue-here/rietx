- **A budget now stops a k-grid search inside a stratum** (issue #795). `score_kgrid` and `kgrid_null` read their `budget` before every block of 2048 k, not only between strata, so a deadline bounds a stratum of tens of thousands of k too. A stratum stopped part-way is reported in the new `strata_cut` field of `KGridScores` and `KNull`, as (name, points scored, points in the stratum), and `coverage` says so in words. A search the budget does not cut returns what it did before, bit for bit.
