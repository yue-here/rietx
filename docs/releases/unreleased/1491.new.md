- An equation fits one parameter's trajectory after a series (WP-2106), as GSAS-II fits its sequential table: `rx.fit_trajectory(series, "phases.0.cell.a", "a0 + k*(T - 600)", variables, coordinates=...)`.
  The `TrajectoryFit` it returns gives each variable two esds, one scaled by χ²/ν and one not, and names the chain it read and the points it left out.
  `read_gsas2_gpx` reads a project's stored sequential equations onto `Gsas2Model.sequential_equations`, rewritten in the expression grammar.
