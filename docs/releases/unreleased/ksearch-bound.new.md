- **`kgrid_bound`: an analytic chance level for a k-grid search** (issue #835). For the k a `score_kgrid` run scored, it returns the score each stratum's k are held to, log₁₀(n_s / (wₛ·α)), so that by Boole's inequality chance reaches it anywhere on them with probability at most α. No shuffles, no oversampling factor and no margin; `alpha` and the `weighting` of α across strata (`"flat"` or `"stratum"`) are the caller's. `score_kvector` and `score_kgrid` take `exclude=`, the windows the peaks were sorted off, and then take each pᵢ on the allowed part of its band, which the bound's guarantee needs; `kgrid_null(exclude_in_score=True)` scores its shuffles the same way. Without these arguments every score is what it was.
