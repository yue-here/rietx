- **A joint fit has the single fit's steering verbs** (WP-2103, #1643).
  `MultiHistogramRefinement` gains `hold`/`unhold`, `tie`/`tie_equal`/`untie` (linear and `expression=`), `add_variable`, `set_values` and `refit`.
  Each names a row as `parameters()` prints it: a shared row bare, a per-pattern row `hist.<h>.`-scoped.
  A bare tie target is tied in every pattern, each to its own source; a tie across patterns is refused, since one number across patterns is a `SharingMap`.
  A held row a stage's glob reached is said once, in `HOLD_BLOCKED_PLAN`, which a joint result now carries.
  `refit` restarts from the answer in one stage, `"restart"`, under the fit's own weights and 2θ limits, and moves a converged fit by no more than 0.01 esd: from this object's last fit, or from `result=`, a saved one.
  A joint fit's `parameters()` marks a tie the caller declared with `TieSpec.user = True`.
