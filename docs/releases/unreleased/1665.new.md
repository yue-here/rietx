- A rigid body can be built from a Z-matrix and refine its bond lengths, bond angles and dihedrals, each with an esd (WP-1814, #759).
  `RigidBody.zmatrix` lists one `ZLine` per atom (its label, or `None` for a dummy), and each line's `r`, `angle` and `dihedral` refine at `phases.i.rigid_bodies.b.zmatrix.k.{r,angle,dihedral}`, in Å and degrees.
  Build one with `bodies.add_body(..., zmatrix=...)`, or over placed atoms with `bodies.zmatrix_body_from_atoms(phase, labels, name, tree=...)`, whose spanning tree you declare.
  A stage refuses at its start an internal the construction cannot carry: a riding H's line, a bend of a linear body, a dihedral at an angle of 0° or 180°, an angle over a collinear reference triple, a dihedral beside a free torsion about its bond, an internal that moves only dummies, an internal that moves an atom of a site body off its site, and an internal that parts a dummy line standing in for a site image from that image while an atom is placed from it.
  A refined length stays above 0 and a refined angle inside (0°, 180°), so a step onto the edge of its box still places every line.
  A restraint on a Z-matrix body raises no `RESTRAINT_ON_FIXED_BODY_GEOMETRY`: its internals can change what the restraint measures, so its row is not a constant.
