- **`SEQUENTIAL_MOMENT_ONSET` is always `info` now, and new codes carry its
  other levels** (WP-2003). The onset bracket used to raise at `warning` when
  its supported side stopped early, when the verdicts switched more than once,
  or under `direction="both"` when the two chains disagreed or one gave no
  verdict. The first three are now `SEQUENTIAL_MOMENT_ONSET_UNRESOLVED`, at
  `warning`, on the same site, and the bracket row stays `info` beside it;
  switching verdicts also give the range the switches span. A chain that gave
  no verdict is `SEQUENTIAL_MOMENT_ONSET_UNCHECKED`, at `info`: the other
  chain's rows keep their level, and its bracket says it was not
  cross-checked and points at that row. Once the two chains are compared, no
  onset row of either chain (`series.backward` included) asks for
  `direction="both"` again. Code that read the onset row's level to decide whether to
  quote the bracket reads `SEQUENTIAL_MOMENT_ONSET_UNRESOLVED` instead. Its
  `value` is `None`: a row the backward chain alone located used to carry that
  bracket's midpoint.
- **`SEQUENTIAL_MOMENT_HOLD` fires only where a pattern's moment is
  unsupported** (WP-2003, #1411). A series whose patterns only gave no verdict
  (a plan that frees no moment, a fit that diverged) no longer raises it: each
  pattern's own status and diagnostics say why. Its three lists of patterns
  name four labels each, then "and N more" and the record that names every
  one.
