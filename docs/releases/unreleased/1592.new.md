- `read_pattern` opens a plain `.zip` of pattern files without extracting it (WP-1315, #134).
  The new format `zip_collection` treats each member another reader claims as one scan, in sorted member-name order, so `scan=k` reads the k-th pattern, and `list_scans` labels each by its member name.
  Directory entries, dotfiles and members no reader claims are skipped and not counted, and so is a member only the ASCII catch-all or a `.cif` suffix claims unless it holds a row of two numbers or a pdCIF data tag (a log, a parameter file, a structure CIF), and reading member 0 without `scan` reports `PATTERN_MULTISCAN_DEFAULTED`.
  The member's own reader decides its σ and axis, and takes the other reader options.
  A stored (uncompressed) zip whose first member is GSAS text is now read as a zip; before, the GSAS reader claimed the archive and failed on its bytes.
  `capabilities().reader_formats` gains `zip_collection`, after `brml`.
