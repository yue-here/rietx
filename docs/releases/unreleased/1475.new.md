- A result carries its whole parameter correlation matrix, and it can be written to a file (WP-1990, #992).
  `RefinementResult.identifiability.correlation` holds every pair's ρ, the matrix the five `top_correlations` are cut from, and a pair the data do not determine is `None`, never a number.
  `Refinement.write_correlation_table(path)`, or `rietx.io.exporters.write_correlation_table`, writes it as a labelled square CSV.
  A joint multi-histogram fit carries the joint solve's matrix, and `for_histogram(h)` gives each pattern's view its own block, spelled as its parameters are.
