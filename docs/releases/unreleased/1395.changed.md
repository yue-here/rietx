- **The GUI's Series panel showed the wrong fit for every pattern after a failed one in 1.11.0** (#1396, fixed in #1395).
  The GUI's server indexed the session's results by position and the staged members by their chain index, and the two disagree after a failed pattern, so each member after one showed its neighbour's fit.
  A series with no failed pattern was never affected, and the library's `SeriesResult` was right throughout.
  If you looked at a series with a failure in the 1.11.0 GUI, look at it again here.
