### Numbers that move

- **A fit that frees an FCJ axial ratio sitting on zero now returns a different
  answer.** A stage that newly frees S/L or H/L at exactly zero starts S/L at
  0.02 and H/L at 0.01 (a start that would equal the other ratio's value takes
  the other number), and records the rows in `StageResult.seeded`. Before, the
  column at zero was nearly zero and a Gauss-Newton fit could end `converged`
  on zero with an esd of order 1 (#1075; WP-1955). On #1075's repro under
  `lab_bragg_brentano`, S/L went from 0.00008 ± 1.29 (Rwp 0.0831) to
  0.04531 ± 0.00018 (Rwp 0.0630). The aperture columns themselves are exact
  now at one zero aperture, so an esd moves where it was read off them (55×
  narrower on the nonzero aperture of `test_e8`). Fits that start both ratios
  positive are unchanged to under 0.02 esd on LaB6, FAP and cpd-1a. In a joint
  fit the two histograms of one instrument may end on opposite orders of the
  pair, which the pair's symmetry allows.
